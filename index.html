About your tutor

Hi, I'm Ciarán.

I’m a native American English speaker. I studied English at Columbia University and hold a CELTA, which is the difference between knowing a language and knowing how to teach it. Most of my students aren’t trying to sound smarter — they’re trying to sound like themselves, clearly, in a language that isn’t their first.

A lot of tutoring is really just careful reading — noticing what a sentence is trying to do, then helping it get there. That’s the part I enjoy most, and it’s what we’ll spend our time on.
